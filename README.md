# Mins & Max vs. UniCorp

***Plant. Water. Harvest. Scale.***

**The "Unicorn" startup Unicorp has come for Max's Family Farm**

Following the sudden death of her beloved Grandfather,

Max has been squeezed out of her family farm by the ruthless  **UniCorp**. 

Now, to reclaim what’s hers, Max has  15 days  to utilize Unicorps new Min technology to scale her farms operations, and make enough money to buy her Grandpas farm back and beat Unicorp at their own game. 

Can Max adapt her farming skills fast enough,  to become the next 100x Agentic Farming Unicorn?
Or will she crumble under Unicorp's insatiable need for ever growing efficiency and productivity and be left behind in this new world?

### Gameplay
*   **Orchestrate:** You aren't just farming; you are managing a fleet of Mins. Deploy them to plant, water, harvest, and clear land. 
*   **Scale:** Buy tools, recruit more Mins, and upgrade your infrastructure at the shop to increase efficiency.
*   **Balance:** Every action counts. Manage your budget, your crops, and your precious time to meet the demands of your tasks before the day (and the deadline) ends.
*   **Efficiency is Key:** The more you automate, the faster you can complete your tasks. But watch out—everything comes at a cost!

### How to Play
*   **Movement:** Use the **WASD** to navigate your farm.
*   **Select Tools:** Use the **Number Keys (1-5)** to swap between your tools and your Mins.
*   **Interact:** Press **Spacebar** to interact with the world, or characters, collect items, or harvest, 
*   **Cursor Action:** Use your mouse to aim and click to deploy Mins or use tools at a specific distance.
*   **Objective:** Follow your task list and manage your farm resources to pay your way back your Grandfathers loans before the final day arrives.

**Hint:**
* Min - Auto Collect + Cut  + Fish
* Blue Min - Min + Auto Water Crops
* Rainbow Min - Blue Min  + Auto Plant Seeds
